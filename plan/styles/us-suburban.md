# US suburban pack

## Roof

- Gabled and hipped dominate at all eras; cross-gables on L and T footprints
  (from the skeleton).
- Pitch 18 – 35°; overhang larger than in Germany (0.4 – 0.7 m).
- Asphalt shingle colours: charcoal, dark brown, grey, weathered green.

## Walls and palette

Siding in white, beige, grey, pale blue, sage, pale yellow; brick red for
older eras; modern farmhouse white with black trim at the new end. Trim
(corner boards, window frames) is white unless the wall is white.

## Facade tiles

| Tile | Notes |
|---|---|
| blank | siding lines are not drawn; colour only |
| window_sash | framed, 2 panes stacked, optional shutters |
| window_picture | wide, living room on the ground floor |
| window_bay | boxed out bay, older eras |
| door_main | with porch slab and posts at old and mid eras |
| door_garage | attached garages are part of the house facade: a ground-row tile, 1 – 2 doors wide |
| porch | ground row run of cells with posts and a low roof |

Rules: symmetric street wall is likely (0.6) at old eras; the garage door sits
at one end of the street wall; dormers are common above 1.5-storey houses.
