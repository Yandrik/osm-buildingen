# Non-residential types

Shared between regions; palettes and a few weights differ.

## Office

- Old (era < 0.4): punched windows with wide piers, more blank wall, stone or
  brick colours, cornice, flat or low hipped roof.
- Mid: horizontal window bands, concrete greys and beiges.
- New: curtain wall panels, large glass share, thin frames, coloured accents.
- Ground floor: taller level, main entrance with canopy, optional stores.
- Multi-part outlines of similar height are solved as one facade.

## Skyscraper

Office rules plus: podium (first 1 – 3 levels wider rhythm), shaft with a
strict vertical grid, crown (setback or parapet block). Vertical fins as an
option at mid eras, full glass at new.

## Industrial (factory, storage hall)

Large blank panels in metal greys, blues, greens; a base band; roller doors
and loading docks on the street wall; small window strip under the eave;
flat, low gabled or sawtooth (later) roof; skylight strips.

## Station

- Bus: one-level pavilion or canopy on posts, glass walls, flat roof.
- Train: 1 – 2 levels, old eras brick with tall arched windows, new eras glass
  and canopy; platform canopy on the rail side when a rail line is in `roads`.

## Store

1 – 2 levels, continuous shopfront on the street wall, sign band above it
(blank coloured band, no text), flat roof with parapet, mostly blank sides.

## Shed

See de-residential.md; the US pack tints it differently.
